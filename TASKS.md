# Grant application product

## Acceptance

- The daemon persists project facts, source documents, consent-bound workflow state and all provider contributions.
- The catalogue supports Turkish and international official directories, refreshing public sources, searching/matching and adding any official programme.
- PDF, DOCX and XLSX imports produce reviewable text and explicit per-file errors. Missing or conflicting facts become questions, never invented answers.
- Claude, Codex and Gemini are required. All three independently research and exchange findings; researcher/writer/reviewer roles remain visible.
- Project sharing scope and every individual provider prompt require human approval after masking.
- Writing waits for confirmed facts, programme requirements and research. Failed providers, unverified references and blocking reviews cannot produce a ready application.
- Application and AI research/debate records export as two real DOCX documents with tables, sources, decisions and audits separated.
- Restart recovery, cancellation, stale approvals, document limits and source-fetch safety fail closed.
